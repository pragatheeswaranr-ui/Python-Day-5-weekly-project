
# Employee Data Analysis – Findings Summary

## 1. Problem Statement

The main objective of this project is to analyse employee data and understand employee distribution, salary patterns and work experience in the company.

## 2. Data Description

This project uses the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset. It contains employee information such as age, department, monthly income and years at company.

## 3. Three Key Insights

1. Department Analysis:
   The bar chart helps identify which department has the highest and lowest number of employees.

2. Monthly Income Analysis:
   The histogram shows how monthly income is distributed among employees.

3. Age and Income Analysis:
   The scatter plot helps understand how monthly income varies across different employee age groups.

## 4. Recommendations

1. The company should regularly review salary distribution across departments to identify possible differences.

2. The company should use employee experience and workforce distribution data for better employee planning.

## 5. Limitations

1. The analysis is based only on the available dataset.
2. The dataset may not represent all organisations.
3. The charts show patterns but cannot explain the exact reasons behind employee salary differences.
