EMPLOYEE DATA ANALYSIS – FINDINGS SUMMARY
1. Problem Statement

The objective of this analysis is to understand employee attrition and identify workplace factors associated with employees leaving the company.

2. Dataset
Dataset: IBM HR Employee Attrition Dataset
Total Employees: 1470
Total Columns: 35
Analysis Tool: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn.
3. Three Key Insights

Insight 1: Employee Attrition

Out of 1470 employees, 237 employees left the company and 1233 employees stayed. The attrition rate is approximately 16.12%.

Insight 2: Overtime and Attrition

Among employees working overtime, 127 employees left the company, while 289 stayed. Employees working overtime show a higher attrition proportion than employees not working overtime.

Insight 3: Monthly Income and Attrition

Employees who left had an average monthly income of 4787, whereas employees who stayed had an average monthly income of 6833.

4. Two Recommendations
The company should review overtime workload and provide better work-life balance for employees.
The company should review salary structures and compensation policies to improve employee satisfaction and retention.
5. Limitations
The analysis is based on the available employee dataset only.
The dataset does not establish the causes of employee attrition.
The analysis shows associations, not cause-and-effect relationships.
Conclusion

The analysis identified differences in attrition based on overtime and monthly income. These findings can help the company understand employee retention patterns and consider improvements in workplace policies.
